Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026. ClipOnce is operated by Fyekicks LLC ("we").

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a scrambled (hashed) password. We never see or store your password in plain text.
  • Billing: payments are handled by Stripe. We keep your Stripe customer id and plan, never your card details.
  • Brands and connected accounts: when you connect YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or Threads to a brand, we store the sign-in tokens that platform gives us, encrypted, plus your channel or account name so you can see what is connected.
  • Clips: videos you upload or links you clip, the clips we make from them, and their captions.

How we use it

Only to run ClipOnce for you: make clips, show them in your account, and post them to the accounts you connected, on the schedule and daily limits you set. We do not sell your data, use it for ads, or share it with anyone except the services that run ClipOnce (Vercel hosting, Neon database, GitHub Actions workers, Stripe billing) and the platforms you post to.

YouTube and Google data

ClipOnce uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies. We request permission to upload videos to your channel and to read your channel's name. We use it only to upload the clips you asked ClipOnce to post and to show which channel is connected. ClipOnce's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove ClipOnce's access at any time in your Google security settings or with Disconnect on your account page.

TikTok data

When you connect TikTok we receive your basic profile (display name and username) and permission to post videos you choose to your account. We use it only to post your clips and show which account is connected. You can disconnect at any time on your account page or in TikTok's settings.

Keeping and deleting data

Disconnecting an account deletes its tokens right away. Deleting a brand deletes its sign-ins and settings. To delete your whole ClipOnce account and its data, contact us and we will do it within 30 days. Clips already posted to your social accounts stay there until you remove them.

Security

Sign-in tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) and only ever used by our own servers. Connections use HTTPS.

Children

ClipOnce is not for anyone under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any ClipOnce email or the address on your Stripe receipt, or write from your account email.
