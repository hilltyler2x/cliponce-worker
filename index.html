Questions

Good questions.

What links can I clip from?

Twitch live streams, past broadcasts, clips and channel links work in the cloud today. You can also upload any video file you own. More platforms are on the way.

How does it pick the moments?

It scores every part of the video using chat activity (message speed, emotes, "clip it" calls), audio energy like laughter and shouting, and the transcript. The highest scoring stretches become clips, trimmed so they start before the moment and end after the payoff.

What is a credit?

1 credit is 1 minute of source video processed. A 2 hour stream uses 120 credits no matter how many clips come out of it. Posting never costs credits.

Will I be charged today?

No. Every paid plan starts with 30 days free. You are only charged after the trial ends, and you can cancel before that in your account page.

Can I clip other people's streams?

Only clip content you own or have permission to use, and always credit the creator. ClipOnce only clips from original streams and videos, never from other clip pages' reposts.

What quality are the clips?

Full HD 1080x1920 vertical MP4, ready for TikTok, Shorts and Reels. Paid plans have no watermark.
